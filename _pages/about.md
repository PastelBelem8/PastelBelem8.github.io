I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine, advised by [Sameer Singh](https://sameersingh.org/) and [Padhraic Smyth](https://ics.uci.edu/~smyth/index.html). My research evaluates the robustness and reliability of large language models—specifically, where and how they fail. I study **gender bias** in models that appear neutral on the surface, **hallucinations** in multi-document summarization, and **uncertainty communication**: whether models accurately express what they know and don't know, and whether humans and other models can correctly interpret those signals.

Before my PhD, I spent two years as a research data scientist at Feedzai, working on algorithmic fairness and explainable AI for fraud detection. I have also interned at Megagon Labs and Capital One, working on LLM evaluation and customization for factual, readable outputs. My goal across all of this work is to surface the failure modes of modern language technologies and build the tools to address them.
