We investigate whether language models can correctly interpret verbalized expressions of uncertainty (e.g., "probably," "I'm not sure"), finding systematic failures with implications for human-AI communication. This paper received the Runner-Up Best Short Paper award at TrustNLP @ NAACL 2024. (* denotes joint first authorship)
